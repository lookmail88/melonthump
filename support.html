MelonThump Support

MelonThump judges watermelon ripeness from the sound of a knock — thump the melon, record five seconds, and get the verdict: unripe, ripe, or overripe. The analysis runs entirely on your iPhone; your recordings never leave the device.

How to get a good reading

  • Hold the phone close to the melon and thump firmly with a knuckle or fingertip, like knocking on a door.
  • Thump once, right after tapping the record button, and keep other noise down during the five seconds.
  • Try two or three spots on the melon if the first verdict is "Couldn't tell".

Credits and ads

New installs start with 5 free analyses. Each completed analysis uses one credit ("Couldn't tell" results are free). When credits run out, you can earn more by watching a short rewarded ad. During launch promotions, a single ad at the start of a session may unlock unlimited thumps until you leave the app.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound recorded? Make sure microphone access is allowed in iOS Settings > MelonThump > Microphone.
  • An ad won't load? Check your connection and try again in a few seconds — the app automatically falls back to a backup ad if the main one fails.
  • Verdict seems off? Every melon knocks differently. Treat the result as a knowledgeable friend's opinion — and thump again in a quieter spot.
