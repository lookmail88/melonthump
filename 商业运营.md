## 1. Positioning 项目定位

- **Product**: an iOS app that judges watermelon ripeness by sound — thump the melon, record, and get an unripe / ripe / overripe verdict from on-device analysis.

- **产品**：拍一拍西瓜、听声辨生熟的 iOS App（录音 → 本地分析 → 生/熟/过熟判定）。

- **Origin**: a father–daughter project — Stephanie owns the UI design (the watermelon and cat characters are the brand assets), Xuyang owns architecture, Pepper writes code and runs deployment; the domain stephaniegao.me is registered in Stephanie's name.

- **出身**：父女合作项目 — Stephanie 负责 UI 设计（西瓜与猫的原创形象即品牌资产），用户负责架构，Pepper 负责开发与部署；域名 stephaniegao.me 以 Stephanie 名义注册。

- **Target users**: household shoppers buying melons (picking a good melon is a universal pain point, and the result is instantly verifiable).

- **目标用户**：买瓜的家庭消费者（挑瓜痛点普遍、结果即时可验证）。

- **Differentiators**: verdicts run fully on the phone (offline, no waiting); every verdict can be followed by a "cut it open and check" contribution, forming a flywheel of real labelled data.

- **差异点**：判定全在手机本地（离线可用、无等待）；每次判定可回传"切开验证"结果，形成真实标注数据飞轮。

## 3. Business Model (Direction Set, Not Yet Implemented) 商业模式（已定方向，未实施）

**Credit economy** (lands with the server phase; design in Technical Development §5):

**Credit 经济**（服务端实施时落地，设计见技术文档 §5）：

- Each analysis costs 1 credit.

- 每次分析消耗 1 credit。

- Out of credits: watching a 30-second ad automatically earns +1 credit (the monetisation channel for free users).

- 无 credit 时：看 30 秒广告自动 +1 credit（免费用户的变现通道）。

- Contribution loop: uploading a cut-open photo + the real verdict for an already-analysed melon earns credit — it drives engagement and accumulates training data at once; the reward amount is TBD (the design draft proposes +1, configurable). The per-ad reward will be carried by the version policy's `creditPerAd` field (−1 = unlimited use that day, no ads shown).

- 贡献回路：对已分析的瓜回传切开照片 + 真实结论（标注贡献）奖励 credit — 既促活又攒训练数据；奖励额度待定（设计稿建议 +1，可配置）。每个广告的奖励数将由版本策略的 `creditPerAd` 字段下发（−1 表示当天无限使用、不显示广告）。

Later options (undecided): an ad-free / family subscription, credit packs as in-app purchases.

后续可选（未决策）：订阅去广告 / 家庭包、credit 礼包内购。

## 4. Operations Plan 运营计划

**Phase 1: Calibration (current)**

**阶段一：校准（当前）**

- Goal: replace the placeholder thresholds with thresholds calibrated on real data.

- 目标：把占位阈值换成真实数据校准过的阈值。

- Actions: use the app heavily through our own melon season, contributing a cut-open photo and the true verdict every time (first labelled batch, target 100 samples); regress the analysis pipeline (thresholds, gate parameters) on the samples.

- 动作：自家买瓜季内高频使用，逐次回传切开照片与真实生熟（攒第一批标注样本，目标先到 100 例）；用样本回归分析管线（阈值、门控参数）。

- Exit criteria: three-class accuracy on our own test set reaches an acceptable level (target ≥ 85%) before entering Phase 2.

- 出口标准：熟/过熟/生三类判定准确率在自测集上达到可接受水平（目标 ≥85%）再进阶段二。

**Phase 2: Small-scale distribution**

**阶段二：小范围分发**

- TestFlight invitations for friends, family and community households; collect recordings from real environments (different phones, supermarket / market noise) and their labels.

- TestFlight 邀请亲友与社区家庭试用，回收真实环境录音（不同手机、超市/市场噪音环境）与标注。

- Exercise the forced-update path (ship a minor update, watch old-version blocking and the upgrade flow).

- 验证强制更新链路（发一版小更新，观察旧版拦截与升级路径）。

- Exit criteria: crash rate and UNKNOWN rate (rejected as too noisy) are stable, and the sample count reaches the ~500 range.

- 出口标准：崩溃率与 UNKNOWN 率（太吵被拒）稳定，样本量到 500 例量级。

**Phase 3: App Store launch and cold start**

**阶段三：App Store 上架与冷启动**

- Launch prep: fill in `updateUrl`; store assets reuse Stephanie's watermelon/cat visuals (brand consistency); privacy label — "recordings never leave the phone" is a selling point, leading with "offline & private".

- 上架准备：填 `updateUrl`、商店素材复用 Stephanie 的西瓜/猫视觉（品牌一致性）、隐私标签（录音不离机是卖点，主打"离线、隐私"）。

- Cold start: word of mouth in family and school communities; summer (melon season) is the natural window — timing aligns with the northern-hemisphere May–August season.

- 冷启动：家庭与学校社区口碑传播；夏季（瓜季）是天然窗口，节奏对齐北半球 5–8 月。

- Content: short "app verdict vs cut-open reality" thump-comparison videos are the most direct shareable material.

- 内容：拍瓜对比短视频（App 判定 vs 切开实况）是最直观的传播素材。

**Long term: the data asset**

**长期：数据资产**

- Labelled samples (audio + cut-open photo + true verdict) keep accumulating into an ML training set; when the heuristic thresholds are one day upgraded to a trained model, this is the moat later entrants cannot buy.

- 标注样本（音频 + 切开照片 + 真实结论）持续积累为 ML 训练集；启发式阈值将来升级为训练模型时，这是后来者买不到的壁垒。
