## 1. Background 立项背景

1. The three-band verdict (unripe / ripe / overripe) is production-ready in 1.2.0: analyzer v2.4 answered 21/21 knocks on a single melon, all correct, backed by cut-open ground truth, and ships with 1.2.0 today.
   1.2.0 已把三档判定（生/熟/过熟）做到可用：分析器 v2.4 在同瓜 21 拍上 21/21 应答且全对（有切开真值背书），今日随 1.2.0 发版。
2. Real use exposed the ceiling of three bands: quality inside the "ripe" band varies widely — melon No. 2 is crisp and sweet, melon No. 1 is sweet but mealy-leaning, a clearly perceptible gap — while their dominant frequencies are nearly identical (medians 208.4 vs 210.4 Hz). The user's need has moved from "is it ripe" to "how good is it".
   真机使用暴露了三档的上限：同一档（熟）内部的品质差异巨大——2 号瓜脆甜、1 号瓜甜但偏面，口感明显分层，而两只瓜的主频几乎相同（中位 208.4 vs 210.4 Hz）。用户需求从"熟没熟"进阶到"好不好"。
3. Decision (user, 2026-11-06): 2.0 is initiated, centered on a **scoring mode**; documentation is prepared first, implementation is decided later.
   用户 2026-11-06 拍板：2.0 立项，核心是**打分模式**；先行准备文档，代码后议。

4. Version-line positioning (user decision, 2026-11-06 15:19): **1.2.0 is the basic stable release** — the foundation (analyzer v2.4, file-based models, menu structure, server-driven policy) is set; **2.0 is the long-term stable release** — scoring and photo fusion complete the feature set, after which the line is maintenance and small iterations; **3.0 is the data-tuned release** — model accuracy tuned on the calibration and co-built data accumulated during the 2.0 era. The 2.0 data pipeline (§5) is therefore the fuel line for 3.0.
   版本线定位（用户 2026-11-06 15:19 拍板）：**1.2.0 为基础稳定版**——地基（分析器 v2.4、模型文件化、菜单结构、服务端策略）定型；**2.0 为长期稳定版**——打分与拍照融合补齐功能面，其后以维护和小步迭代为主；**3.0 为数据调优版**——用 2.0 阶段攒下的标定与共建数据调优模型精度。故 §5 的数据管线即 3.0 的燃料线。

## 2. Goals and Scope 目标与范围

1. **Scoring mode (core)**: beyond the three-band verdict, give each melon a quality score from 0–100. The score must reflect texture gradations inside the ripe band (crisp vs mealy, sweetness, etc.), not merely re-map the three bands onto numbers.
   **打分模式（核心）**：在三档判定之外，给每只瓜一个 0–100 的品质分数。分数须反映熟带内部的质地分层（脆/面、甜度等），而不只是把三档换算成分。
2. **Photo feature (formal, user decision 2026-11-06 12:35)**: 2.0 includes photo capture — rind appearance/texture photos, fused with acoustic features into the score; photo samples are collected from the start of the calibration phase. What to photograph is decided (12:37): **rind texture close-ups primary, a whole-melon shot secondary**; the fusion validation requirement is in §6.
   **拍照功能（正式纳入，2026-11-06 12:35 用户拍板）**：2.0 包含拍照——拍瓜皮外观/纹理，与声学特征融合参与打分；标定阶段即同步采集照片样本。拍什么已定（12:37）：**瓜皮纹理近摄为主、整瓜外观为辅**；融合的验证要求见 §6。
3. **Out of scope for 2.0**: external audio capture hardware (user decision 2026-11-06: defer; re-evaluate only if signal-to-noise becomes the bottleneck); any charging beyond model fees (the pricing rule is unchanged: model fees only).
   **不在 2.0 范围**：外部音频采集硬件（用户 2026-11-06 定：等以后，信噪比成为瓶颈时再评估）；订阅或任何模型费之外的收费（收费口径不变：只收模型费）。
4. The scoring model ships free in 2.0; the first paid model is reserved for 3.0 data-tuned model (§7 item 6, decided 2026-11-08).
   打分模型在 2.0 免费发布；首个付费模型留给 3.0 的数据调优模型（§7 第 6 条，2026-11-08 定）。
5. **User participation in improving model quality (raised by the user 2026-11-06 12:42, included in the 2.0 plan)**: open a voluntary contribution channel in the app — users may submit their own complete samples (appearance / sound / cut-open photo / feedback) to model iteration, forming a data flywheel of "the more people use it, the more accurate the model becomes". Data tiers and the privacy stance are in §5.6 and §7.
   **用户参与提升模型品质（2026-11-06 12:42 用户提出，纳入 2.0 规划）**：App 内开通自愿贡献通道——用户可提交自己的全样本（外观 / 声音 / 切开图 / 反馈）参与模型迭代，形成"用的人越多、模型越准"的数据飞轮。数据分级与隐私口径见 §5.6 与 §7。
6. **Owner ad exemption (user decision, 2026-11-06 15:24)**: the next release after 1.2.0 (2.0, or an earlier 1.2.x patch) adds a server-side exemption list — the client sends its install_id with the version-policy request (new optional header), the server checks it against a server-configured allowlist and returns an `adExempt` boolean in the policy response; exempt devices skip the ad gate entirely, in and after the promo period. The allowlist itself never appears in any response; it initially holds only the owner's own device(s). Extended 2026-11-07: the list is maintained from the admin page (§2 item 7) and may also hold contributors granted permanent ad exemption; contributors may alternatively be granted a countable ad-free allowance, likewise granted and managed from the admin page and delivered through the policy. Backward compatible: 1.2.0 clients neither send the header nor read the field, so their behavior is unchanged.
   **机主广告豁免（用户 2026-11-06 15:24 拍板）**：1.2.0 之后的下个版本（2.0，或更早的 1.2.x 补丁版）加服务端豁免名单——客户端请求版本策略时带上自己的 install_id（新增可选请求头），服务端与服务端配置的名单比对，在策略响应里回一个 `adExempt` 布尔值；命中的设备完全跳过广告门（推广期与推广后都跳）。名单本身永不出现在任何响应里；初始只放机主自己的设备。2026-11-07 扩容：名单由管理页维护（§2 第 7 条），也可纳入被授予永久免广告的贡献者；贡献者也可改为授予可计数的免广告次数，同样在管理页发放管理、经策略下发。向后兼容：1.2.0 客户端不发这个头、也不读这个字段，行为不变。
7. **Server admin page (user decision, 2026-11-07)**: 2.0 adds a server-side web admin console for operating everything the server manages: version-policy parameters (e.g. `initialFreeThumps`, promo window, force-update flags), the `adExempt` allowlist (§2 item 6), review and moderation of contributed samples (§5 item 6) — including browsing the contributed materials themselves (appearance photos, recordings, cut-open photos, scores and feedback), contributor and sample bookkeeping, and usage-stats summaries. It also operates per-user switches: reviewing contributor applications (approve or decline, at the owner's discretion) and enabling or disabling a specific user's contributor status, and granting contributors ad-free allowances (a managed count) or permanent ad exemption (§2 item 6). Until it ships, these are managed via config and database edits; the admin page replaces that with an auditable UI. Its scope and access control are an open question (§7 item 11). **Focus (clarified by the user, 2026-11-07): the admin page is mainly about two things — advertising** (the exemption list, ad-free allowances, promo and free-day switches) **and contributor/material management** (contributor status, sample review and the materials themselves). **Ordinary users are explicitly not managed**: no per-user operations on them; general policy parameters and stats summaries are supporting functions, not the core. Contributor records display the note each contributor left at enrollment (§5 item 6), so the owner knows who they are. **Client requirements: the admin page is responsive — fully usable on phone and desktop — and must work in Safari, Chrome and Edge (current mainstream versions).** **API style (user decision, 2026-11-07)**: the admin page uses REST endpoints (dedicated admin endpoints, separate from the client /api/v1); GraphQL was considered and deferred — simplicity first. **Delivery form (user decision, 2026-11-07)**: the owner console is this standalone web page, not an in-app section — it iterates without App Store review and keeps the consumer app clean. ## 3. Scientific Basis 科学依据（2026-11-06 文献调研要点）

1. Acoustic non-destructive testing of produce (acoustic impulse response) is an established field; watermelon is one of its main study subjects.
   学科背景：农产品声学无损检测（声脉冲响应法）是成熟方向，西瓜为主要研究对象之一。
2. Consensus findings: resonance frequency falls as ripeness advances, decay rate rises; four acoustic feature families have been compared in the literature — resonance peak frequency, decay time, waveform symmetry, and band magnitudes.
   共识结论：共振频率随成熟下降、衰减率随成熟上升；文献比较过的声学特征有四类——共振峰频率、衰减时间、波形对称度、频带幅值。
3. The firmness index f²·m (frequency squared × mass) correlates with watermelon firmness at r≈0.74 → **frequency must be mass-normalized before it can be compared across melons**.
   硬度指数 f²·m（频率平方 × 质量）与西瓜硬度相关 r≈0.74 → **频率必须用质量归一化后才可跨瓜比较**。
4. Accuracy references: three-band classification with feature engineering (HHT+NMF) reaches 92.2% (300 melons); a 2025 phone-capture study confirms variety, knock position (the equator discriminates best), force, and device model are all significant variables.
   精度参考：三档分类经特征工程（HHT+NMF）可达 92.2%（300 只瓜）；手机采集研究（2025）确认品种、敲击部位（赤道部区分度最高）、力度、设备型号均为显著变量。
5. Fusion evidence: a 2026 Thailand study (400 melons) shows acoustic features plus mass classify maturity, while rind image texture is indispensable for estimating sugar content (SSC); domestic research likewise shows audio + spectral fusion beats any single signal.
   融合证据：泰国 2026 年研究（400 只瓜）表明声学特征 + 质量管成熟度分类、瓜皮图像纹理对糖度（SSC）估计不可或缺；国内研究亦表明音频 + 光谱融合优于单一信号。
6. Published frequency bands disagree across studies (variety/size/method differences); there is no universal standard → 2.0 keeps the project's own calibration route, anchored on its own ground-truth samples.
   各家标定的频率带互不一致（品种/尺寸/方法差异），无统一标准 → 2.0 沿用自家标定路线，以自有真值样本为准。

## 4. Core Technical Constraints 核心技术约束（自有数据实证）

1. **Dominant frequency cannot separate texture** (hard constraint): melons No. 1 and No. 2 differ by only ~2 Hz in median dominant frequency with heavily overlapping distributions, yet differ by 10+ points in quality. Scoring features must include texture dimensions: decay characteristics, spectral shape, crest distribution, and the like.
   **主频分不出质地**（硬约束）：1、2 号瓜主频中位仅差 2 Hz、分布大量重叠，品质却差 10 分以上。打分特征必须包含质地维度：衰减特性、谱形、crest 分布等。
2. Contact-resonance artifacts (the ~558 Hz class) come from the knock chain; v2.4 already sidesteps them with the persistent spectrum. Scoring feature extraction must inherit that artifact resistance and must not fall back to instantaneous-spectrum definitions.
   接触共振伪成分（~558 Hz 一类）由敲击链路产生，v2.4 已用持久谱绕开；打分的特征提取须继承该抗伪成分能力，不得退回瞬时谱口径。
3. Knock force is a two-way trap (hard knocks clip, soft knocks lack SNR); calibration capture must follow a single protocol (§5).
   力度是双向陷阱（重敲削波、轻敲信噪比不足）；标定采集须按统一协议（§5）控制。
4. Analysis stays fully on-device (the project's standing architecture is unchanged); the scoring model ships through the same .ml model-file mechanism.
   分析仍全在 iPhone 本地（项目既定架构不变）；打分模型沿用 .ml 模型文件机制投放。

## 6. Validation Gates 验证门

1. Scoring gate: on held-out samples, the model's mean absolute error against user scores and its ranking consistency must meet the bar before release (threshold to be fixed; candidate: MAE ≤ 8 points).
   打分验证门：留出样本上，模型分数与用户评分的平均绝对误差与排序一致性达标才上线（具体阈值待定，候选：MAE ≤ 8 分）。
2. Image-fusion gate: the photo feature is confirmed in scope; this gate governs **the fusion scoring caliber** — on same-melon samples (photo + knocks + cut-open truth), blind-tested, fused scoring must measurably beat acoustic-only scoring for the score to ship in the fused caliber; if it does not win, photos are still collected and displayed, but the score uses the acoustic caliber only, with the reason recorded honestly.
   图像融合验证门：拍照功能已确定纳入，本门管的是**融合打分的口径**——同瓜样本（照片 + 拍击 + 切开真值）盲测，融合打分须可测地胜过纯声学打分，才以融合口径上线打分；若不胜，照片仍照常采集与展示，但分数只走声学口径，并如实记录原因。
3. The three-band verdict must not regress: shipping scoring does not change v2.4's existing three-band acceptance baseline.
   三档判定不得回退：打分上线不改变 v2.4 三档的既有验收基线。

Variety scope: **decided (2026-11-08) — no variety restriction; samples of any variety are accepted** (contributed samples cannot be variety-controlled anyway, and refusing them would throttle the flywheel). **Variety is captured by selection, never by fill-in (user decision: choices, not typing): the capture flow offers a preset list — seedless, seeded, mini / personal-size, yellow-flesh, Kirin, Black Beauty — plus an other / not-sure choice; the fallback is itself a choice, and there is no free-text variety field anywhere** — a controlled vocabulary keeps per-variety stratification clean at modeling time. In-house calibration runs the other way: it concentrates on the most common retail variety to anchor one variety firmly. If inter-variety differences prove large at modeling time, the scope may tighten later; the collected data is not wasted. This question is closed.
   品种范围：**已定（2026-11-08）——不限品种、全收**（共建样本的品种本来就控制不了，拒收等于掐飞轮）。**品种只点选、绝不填写（用户拍板：给选择不要填）：采集流程给出预置列表——无籽瓜、有籽瓜、迷你瓜、黄瓤瓜、麒麟瓜、黑美人——外加「其他/不清楚」一项；兜底本身也是点选项，全程没有品种自由输入框**——受控词表让建模时按品种分层干净。自家标定反着来：集中打市面最常见的品种，把一个品种的锚点打牢。将来建模若发现品种间差异大，再收紧不迟，已攒数据不浪费。本条关闭。
8. Privacy stance for user contributions: the entry-point proposal is on hold (§5.6). **Contributor identity is decided (2026-11-07): a dedicated anonymous contributor UUID generated at opt-in and kept in the Keychain (§5.6).** **The remainder is decided (2026-11-08): (i) uploaded fields are limited to the sample package itself — appearance photos, recordings, cut-open photo, score / taste / band-truth feedback, the bookkeeping metadata (variety by selection, weight, source, date), and the contributor ID; no names, contacts or device identifiers, and the enrollment note is never part of sample data (owner-only, §5.6); (ii) materials are stored on the project own server storage (no third-party cloud); when a contributor withdraws or deletes a submission in self-service, the server copies are truly deleted, with backups aging out within 30 days; (iii) the App Store privacy label declares photos and audio as collected, linked only to the anonymous contributor ID, used for model improvement, never for tracking and never shared with third parties — and the in-app explanation uses the same wording. The entry-point proposal remains on hold; this question is otherwise closed.**
   用户共建的隐私口径：入口提案暂缓待议（§5.6）。**贡献者身份已定（2026-11-07）：开通时生成专属匿名贡献者 UUID、存 Keychain（§5.6）。****余项已定（2026-11-08）：（一）上传字段限死为样本包本身——外观照、录音、切开图、评分/口感/三档真值反馈、簿记字段（品种点选、重量、来源、日期）与贡献者 ID；不含姓名、通讯录、设备标识，开通留言永不进样本数据（仅机主可见，§5.6）；（二）素材存自家服务器存储（不上第三方云）；贡献者在自助里撤回或删除提交，服务端副本真删、备份 30 天内自然过期；（三）App Store 隐私标签如实申报——收集照片与音频、仅关联匿名贡献者 ID、用途为改进模型、不追踪、不与第三方共享，App 内说明用同一套话。入口提案仍暂缓；本条余项关闭。**
9. Contribution incentives: **one instrument is decided (2026-11-07) — ad-free privileges granted and managed from the admin page: a countable ad-free allowance, or permanent ad exemption (§2 items 6–7).** **The remainder is decided (2026-11-08): earning is anchored on approved complete samples only — each approved complete sample earns 5 ad-free units (one unit waives one rewarded ad to unlock a session), an initial parameter the admin page can adjust. Incomplete samples earn nothing; a sample that is returned, fixed and then approved earns normally (a return is a rework note, not a penalty). Ad-free privileges are the only incentive currency — no credits and no models are stacked on top. The contribution ledger and the Invite credit economy are separate books: neither converts into the other. This question is closed.**
   贡献激励：**一种手段已定（2026-11-07）——由管理页发放管理的免广告权益：可计数的免广告次数，或永久免广告（§2 第 6–7 条）。****余项已定（2026-11-08）：发放只认审核通过的全样本——每通过 1 份全样本发 5 次免广告（1 次＝免看一支激励广告解锁一个会话），为初始参数、管理页可调。残缺样本不发；退回后补正再通过的照常发（退回是返工单、不是惩罚）。激励只用免广告一种货币，不叠加 credit 或模型。与 Invite 两套账、互不兑换。本条关闭。**
10. Review and anti-gaming for contributed data: who reviews, and how are volume-farming and fake feedback identified and removed? **Decided (2026-11-08): review is two-tier. Pepper runs a preliminary review on every submission — mechanical completeness (all four parts present, bookkeeping fields filled), hash-based duplicate detection on photos and recordings, consistency checks (the submitted recording is run through the analyzer and compared against the claimed band and score range; the cut-open photo is eyeballed for gross mismatch as an advisory flag only), and anomaly flags (submission bursts, self-contradictory tags) — and attaches a recommendation to each sample in the admin page: suggest-approve, suggest-return with a drafted reason, or flag-for-owner. Pepper preliminary actions are written to the audit log; every final decision (approve / return / remove) is the owner alone. Removal means void-marking, not physical deletion: the record stays for audit, but a voided sample counts neither toward modeling nor toward the 30-sample threshold, and ad-free units it earned are clawed back (unused balance only, floored at zero). Repeat offenders lose contributor status (owner revocation, §5.6). Taste feedback is captured as selectable preset tags (multi-select — e.g. crisp, mealy, sandy, juicy, sweet, bland), never free text, under the same choices-not-typing rule as variety (§7 item 7). This question is closed.**
   共建数据审核与防刷：谁来审、刷量/假反馈如何识别与剔除？**已定（2026-11-08）：审核分两级。Pepper 对每份提交做初审——机械完整度（四部分齐全、簿记字段齐全）、素材哈希去重（照片与录音）、一致性核查（把提交的录音跑一遍分析、与自报三档及评分档位比对；切开图仅作大体相符性的肉眼提示、只作标记）、异常标记（密集提交、标签自相矛盾）——并在管理页给每份样本挂一条初审意见：建议通过 / 建议退回（附原因草稿）/ 存疑待机主看。初审动作进审计日志；一切终审决定（通过 / 退回 / 剔除）只由机主下。剔除＝标记作废、不物理删除：记录留痕，但作废样本不计入建模、也不计入 30 只门槛，其已挣的免广告次数追回未用部分（余额最低到 0）。屡犯者撤销贡献者资格（机主权力，§5.6）。口感反馈改标签点选（预置标签多选，如脆 / 面 / 沙 / 多汁 / 甜 / 淡）、不自由填写，与品种同一条「给选择不要填」规矩（§7 第 7 条）。本条关闭。**
11. Server admin page (§2 item 7): its center of gravity is decided (ad management and contributor/material management; ordinary users are not managed); still open: the exact first-version feature list within that scope, how access is authenticated (an owner login, or behind the existing Cloudflare Access), and whether it needs an audit log of changes? **Access authentication is decided (2026-11-07): the page sits behind Cloudflare Access (owner email allowlist; no self-built login, and the server stores no password), scoped to the admin surface only — the public /api/v1 the app uses stays outside Access.** **First-version feature list is decided (2026-11-07)**: (A) contributors — application review (approve / decline), a contributor roster with status toggles, and contributor detail showing all their submissions; (B) material review — a sample list filterable by status and by contributor, each row flagged for completeness, sample detail viewing the materials themselves (appearance photos, playable recordings, cut-open photos, scores and feedback) with approve / return, and sample removal; (C) ads — adExempt list management, ad-free grants (counted-balance edits, permanent exemption, revocation), and the ad switches (promo end, per-version first-N allowance, free day); (D) a read-only overview (contributor count, complete vs incomplete sample counts, review backlog). Explicitly NOT in the first version: editing release-class version-policy numbers (they stay on the existing release process), dataset export (deferred to the 3.0 data work), and any per-user operation on ordinary users. **The boundary between the two classes is fixed (decided 2026-11-08): the admin-changeable operational switches are the promo end, the per-version first-N allowance, the free-day switch, the adExempt list, and ad-free grants; release-class numbers — latestVersion and minSupportedVersion (the force-update floor) — are never editable in the admin page and change only through the release process (repository, PR, ArgoCD).** The audit log is decided (2026-11-08): the first version carries a minimal append-only audit log — every admin change records who (the Cloudflare Access identity), when, which object, and the before/after values. With this, every sub-item of this question is closed. **Return-with-reason is decided (2026-11-08): returning a sample in review requires a short reason note, and the contributor sees it in their in-app self-service (§5 item 6) — a return without a reason leaves the contributor guessing what to fix.**
   服务端管理页面（§2 第 7 条）：重心已定（广告管理与素材贡献者管理；普通用户不管）；尚待定：该范围内首版的具体功能清单、访问怎么认证（机主登录，还是挂现有的 Cloudflare Access 之后）、变更要不要留审计日志？**访问认证已定（2026-11-07）：挂 Cloudflare Access（机主邮箱白名单；不自建登录、服务端不存密码），且只圈管理面——App 用的公开 /api/v1 不进 Access。** **首版功能清单已定（2026-11-07）**：（A）贡献者——申请审批（通过 / 不予通过）、贡献者名单与资格开关、贡献者详情（看其全部提交）；（B）素材审核——样本列表可按状态与贡献者筛选、每条带完整度标记，样本详情可看素材本体（外观照、可播放的录音、切开图、评分与反馈）并通过 / 退回，样本可剔除；（C）广告——豁免名单管理、免广告发放（次数增减、永久免广告、收回）、广告开关（推广期截止、每版前 N 次、免费日）；（D）只读概览（贡献者数、全样本 / 残缺样本数、待审积压）。明确不进首版：发布类版本策略数字的编辑（继续走现有发布流程）、数据集导出（推迟到 3.0 数据工作）、对普通用户的任何按人操作。**两类边界写死（2026-11-08 定）：管理页可改的运营开关为——推广期截止、每版前 N 次、免费日开关、豁免名单、免广告发放；发布类数字——latestVersion 与 minSupportedVersion（强制升级下限）——永远不可在管理页改，只走发布流程（仓库、PR、ArgoCD）。**审计日志已定（2026-11-08）：首版带最小版 append-only 审计流水——每笔管理变更记录谁（Cloudflare Access 身份）、何时、改了哪个对象、改前改后。本条全部子项至此关闭。**退回带原因已定（2026-11-08）：审核退回样本必须附一句原因，贡献者在 App 内自助里能看到——不带原因的退回只会让贡献者瞎猜。**
