## 1. Background 立项背景

1. The three-band verdict (unripe / ripe / overripe) is production-ready in 1.2.0: analyzer v2.4 answered 21/21 knocks on a single melon, all correct, backed by cut-open ground truth, and ships with 1.2.0 today.
   1.2.0 已把三档判定（生/熟/过熟）做到可用：分析器 v2.4 在同瓜 21 拍上 21/21 应答且全对（有切开真值背书），今日随 1.2.0 发版。
2. Real use exposed the ceiling of three bands: quality inside the "ripe" band varies widely — melon No. 2 is crisp and sweet, melon No. 1 is sweet but mealy-leaning, a clearly perceptible gap — while their dominant frequencies are nearly identical (medians 208.4 vs 210.4 Hz). The user's need has moved from "is it ripe" to "how good is it".
   真机使用暴露了三档的上限：同一档（熟）内部的品质差异巨大——2 号瓜脆甜、1 号瓜甜但偏面，口感明显分层，而两只瓜的主频几乎相同（中位 208.4 vs 210.4 Hz）。用户需求从"熟没熟"进阶到"好不好"。
3. Decision (user, 2026-11-06): 2.0 is initiated, centered on a **scoring mode**; documentation is prepared first, implementation is decided later.
   用户 2026-11-06 拍板：2.0 立项，核心是**打分模式**；先行准备文档，代码后议。

## 2. Goals and Scope 目标与范围

1. **Scoring mode (core)**: beyond the three-band verdict, give each melon a quality score from 0–100. The score must reflect texture gradations inside the ripe band (crisp vs mealy, sweetness, etc.), not merely re-map the three bands onto numbers.
   **打分模式（核心）**：在三档判定之外，给每只瓜一个 0–100 的品质分数。分数须反映熟带内部的质地分层（脆/面、甜度等），而不只是把三档换算成分。
2. **Photo feature (formal, user decision 2026-11-06 12:35)**: 2.0 includes photo capture — rind appearance/texture photos, fused with acoustic features into the score; photo samples are collected from the start of the calibration phase. What exactly to photograph (whole-melon appearance vs close-up rind texture) is an open question (§7); the fusion validation requirement is in §6.
   **拍照功能（正式纳入，2026-11-06 12:35 用户拍板）**：2.0 包含拍照——拍瓜皮外观/纹理，与声学特征融合参与打分；标定阶段即同步采集照片样本。拍什么（整瓜外观 / 纹理近摄）列开放问题（§7），融合的验证要求见 §6。
3. **Out of scope for 2.0**: external audio capture hardware (user decision 2026-11-06: defer; re-evaluate only if signal-to-noise becomes the bottleneck); any charging beyond model fees (the pricing rule is unchanged: model fees only).
   **不在 2.0 范围**：外部音频采集硬件（用户 2026-11-06 定：等以后，信噪比成为瓶颈时再评估）；订阅或任何模型费之外的收费（收费口径不变：只收模型费）。
4. Whether the scoring model becomes the first paid model is an open question (§7), not pre-decided.
   打分模型与付费模型的关系（是否作为首个付费模型）列为开放问题（§7），不预设。

## 3. Scientific Basis 科学依据（2026-11-06 文献调研要点）

1. Acoustic non-destructive testing of produce (acoustic impulse response) is an established field; watermelon is one of its main study subjects.
   学科背景：农产品声学无损检测（声脉冲响应法）是成熟方向，西瓜为主要研究对象之一。
2. Consensus findings: resonance frequency falls as ripeness advances, decay rate rises; four acoustic feature families have been compared in the literature — resonance peak frequency, decay time, waveform symmetry, and band magnitudes.
   共识结论：共振频率随成熟下降、衰减率随成熟上升；文献比较过的声学特征有四类——共振峰频率、衰减时间、波形对称度、频带幅值。
3. The firmness index f²·m (frequency squared × mass) correlates with watermelon firmness at r≈0.74 → **frequency must be mass-normalized before it can be compared across melons**.
   硬度指数 f²·m（频率平方 × 质量）与西瓜硬度相关 r≈0.74 → **频率必须用质量归一化后才可跨瓜比较**。
4. Accuracy references: three-band classification with feature engineering (HHT+NMF) reaches 92.2% (300 melons); a 2025 phone-capture study confirms variety, knock position (the equator discriminates best), force, and device model are all significant variables.
   精度参考：三档分类经特征工程（HHT+NMF）可达 92.2%（300 只瓜）；手机采集研究（2025）确认品种、敲击部位（赤道部区分度最高）、力度、设备型号均为显著变量。
5. Fusion evidence: a 2026 Thailand study (400 melons) shows acoustic features plus mass classify maturity, while rind image texture is indispensable for estimating sugar content (SSC); domestic research likewise shows audio + spectral fusion beats any single signal.
   融合证据：泰国 2026 年研究（400 只瓜）表明声学特征 + 质量管成熟度分类、瓜皮图像纹理对糖度（SSC）估计不可或缺；国内研究亦表明音频 + 光谱融合优于单一信号。
6. Published frequency bands disagree across studies (variety/size/method differences); there is no universal standard → 2.0 keeps the project's own calibration route, anchored on its own ground-truth samples.
   各家标定的频率带互不一致（品种/尺寸/方法差异），无统一标准 → 2.0 沿用自家标定路线，以自有真值样本为准。

## 4. Core Technical Constraints 核心技术约束（自有数据实证）

1. **Dominant frequency cannot separate texture** (hard constraint): melons No. 1 and No. 2 differ by only ~2 Hz in median dominant frequency with heavily overlapping distributions, yet differ by 10+ points in quality. Scoring features must include texture dimensions: decay characteristics, spectral shape, crest distribution, and the like.
   **主频分不出质地**（硬约束）：1、2 号瓜主频中位仅差 2 Hz、分布大量重叠，品质却差 10 分以上。打分特征必须包含质地维度：衰减特性、谱形、crest 分布等。
2. Contact-resonance artifacts (the ~558 Hz class) come from the knock chain; v2.4 already sidesteps them with the persistent spectrum. Scoring feature extraction must inherit that artifact resistance and must not fall back to instantaneous-spectrum definitions.
   接触共振伪成分（~558 Hz 一类）由敲击链路产生，v2.4 已用持久谱绕开；打分的特征提取须继承该抗伪成分能力，不得退回瞬时谱口径。
3. Knock force is a two-way trap (hard knocks clip, soft knocks lack SNR); calibration capture must follow a single protocol (§5).
   力度是双向陷阱（重敲削波、轻敲信噪比不足）；标定采集须按统一协议（§5）控制。
4. Analysis stays fully on-device (the project's standing architecture is unchanged); the scoring model ships through the same .ml model-file mechanism.
   分析仍全在 iPhone 本地（项目既定架构不变）；打分模型沿用 .ml 模型文件机制投放。

## 5. Calibration Data Plan 标定数据计划（成败关键，先于建模）

1. One sample per melon: multi-knock recordings + **pre-knock appearance photo (rind texture)** + variety + **weight** + purchase source/date + knock-position notes + cut-open photo and description + three-band ground truth + **user score (0–100)**.
   样本单元（一只瓜一份）：多拍录音 + **拍击前外观照（瓜皮纹理）** + 品种 + **重量** + 购买来源/日期 + 敲击部位记录 + 切开照片与描述 + 三档真值 + **用户评分（0–100）**。
2. Anchors already in hand (recordings + cut-open truth + score, the full set):
   已有锚点（录音 + 切开真值 + 评分三件套齐全）：
   - Melon No. 2: **90 or above** (deep red, uniform, crisp and sweet).
     2 号瓜：**90 分以上**（深红均匀、脆甜）；
   - Melon No. 1: **80** (sweet but mealy-leaning).
     1 号瓜：**80 分**（甜但偏面）。
3. To be added: melon No. 3 (~247 Hz) — ask the user to score it after cutting; from then on, bank one sample per melon bought.
   待补：3 号瓜（约 247 Hz）切开后请用户评分入档；后续每买一只瓜顺手攒一份样本。
4. Capture protocol essentials: knock at the equator; moderate force (no clipping as the bar); at least 3 knocks per melon; weigh on a kitchen scale to 10 g (for f²·m normalization).
   采集协议要点：敲赤道部；力度适中（以不削波为准）；每只至少 3 拍；称重用厨房秤，精确到 10 g 即可（用于 f²·m 归一化）。
5. Sample-size threshold (proposal, awaiting user decision): start offline modeling experiments only at ≥30 scored samples; before that, collect data only, no modeling.
   样本量门槛（建议值，待用户定）：带评分样本 ≥30 只后再启动离线建模实验；在此之前只攒数据、不建模。

## 6. Validation Gates 验证门

1. Scoring gate: on held-out samples, the model's mean absolute error against user scores and its ranking consistency must meet the bar before release (threshold to be fixed; candidate: MAE ≤ 8 points).
   打分验证门：留出样本上，模型分数与用户评分的平均绝对误差与排序一致性达标才上线（具体阈值待定，候选：MAE ≤ 8 分）。
2. Image-fusion gate: the photo feature is confirmed in scope; this gate governs **the fusion scoring caliber** — on same-melon samples (photo + knocks + cut-open truth), blind-tested, fused scoring must measurably beat acoustic-only scoring for the score to ship in the fused caliber; if it does not win, photos are still collected and displayed, but the score uses the acoustic caliber only, with the reason recorded honestly.
   图像融合验证门：拍照功能已确定纳入，本门管的是**融合打分的口径**——同瓜样本（照片 + 拍击 + 切开真值）盲测，融合打分须可测地胜过纯声学打分，才以融合口径上线打分；若不胜，照片仍照常采集与展示，但分数只走声学口径，并如实记录原因。
3. The three-band verdict must not regress: shipping scoring does not change v2.4's existing three-band acceptance baseline.
   三档判定不得回退：打分上线不改变 v2.4 三档的既有验收基线。

## 7. Open Questions 开放问题（待用户逐项定）

1. The scoring scale: what anchors 100 points (the user's perfect melon)? What is 0? Is the scale absolute or relative within a batch?
   评分量表：100 分的锚点是什么（用户心中的完美瓜）？0 分对应什么？评分是绝对量表还是"同批相对"？
2. The sample-size threshold: is ≥30 (§5.5) the right bar?
   样本量门槛取多少（§5.5 的 ≥30 是否合适）？
3. How does weight get into the app: manual entry, photographing the scale, or not in the app at all in 2.0 (calibration records only)?
   重量怎么进 App：用户手动输入、拍照读秤、还是 2.0 先不进 App（仅标定阶段记录）？
4. What should the image capture: whole-melon appearance, close-up rind texture, or the cut cross-section (which can only verify after the fact, never predict)?
   图像拍什么：整瓜外观、瓜皮纹理近摄、还是切开剖面（剖面只能事后验证、不能预测）？
5. Score presentation: score alongside the three bands, or score primary with bands secondary?
   打分展示形态：分数与三档并存，还是分数为主、档位为辅？
6. Does the scoring model become the first paid model (pricing rule: model fees only; purchases kept permanently)?
   打分模型是否作为首个付费模型（收费口径：只收模型费；已购永久保留）？
7. Variety scope: calibrate common varieties first (e.g. Black Beauty / Kirin types), or collect across varieties from the start?
   品种范围：先只标定常见品种（如黑美人/麒麟类），还是不限品种混采？
