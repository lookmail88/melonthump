## 1. One-Liner 一句话

Thump a watermelon, record the sound, and let the app judge ripeness in three grades — unripe, ripe, overripe — turning a veteran melon picker's ear into software.

手拍西瓜、录下声音，由 App 判断成熟度三档——生、正好、过熟——把老师傅的耳朵数字化。

## 2. How It Works 原理

The user thumps the melon's equator while the iOS app records; the analysis runs entirely on the phone — FFT extracts the dominant frequency and fixed thresholds classify it: below 160 Hz overripe, 160–280 Hz ripe, above 280 Hz unripe. No audio ever leaves the device.

用户在西瓜赤道部位拍击，iOS App 同步录音；分析全部在手机本地完成——FFT 提取主频，固定阈值分类：低于 160 Hz 为过熟、160–280 Hz 为正好、高于 280 Hz 为生。音频不离开设备。

The classifier began as a server implementation and was ported to Swift line-for-line (same windowing, bands, thresholds and confidence curve), so verdicts are identical on-device.

分类器最初在服务端实现，现已逐行移植为 Swift（同样的加窗、频段、阈值与置信度曲线），本地判定结果与服务端完全一致。

## 3. Scientific Basis 科学依据

As a watermelon ripens, its resonant frequency drops and damping changes: ripe melons sound deep and round, unripe ones higher and crisper, overripe ones dull and muffled.

西瓜成熟过程中共振频率下降、阻尼变化：熟瓜声音低沉浑圆，生瓜偏高偏脆，过熟的发闷发"噗"。

Published studies using phone-recorded thump sounds report 91–98% accuracy (Zeng et al., 2014) and about 86% overall / 93% at the equator (Pintor et al.), so dominant frequency plus damping is a sound basis for a v1 fixed-threshold classifier.

已有研究用手机录音的拍击声区分生熟，准确率达 91–98%（Zeng 等，2014）；另一研究综合准确率约 86%、赤道拍击约 93%（Pintor 等）。因此以主频加阻尼做 v1 固定阈值分类，工程上站得住。

## 5. Product Design 产品设计

### Credit Economy Credit 经济

One analysis costs 1 credit. Users earn credits by watching a 30-second ad (+1) or by contributing ground truth: after cutting the melon open, uploading a photo with the real verdict (+1, configurable). Credits are tracked in an append-only ledger with idempotency keys.

一次分析消耗 1 个 credit。用户可通过看 30 秒广告（+1）或回传标注赚 credit：切开西瓜后上传照片和真实结论（+1，金额可配置）。credit 用只增流水账记录，带幂等键防重。

### Labeling & ML Data 标注与机器学习数据

Every analysis sample and its later user-confirmed label is stored (audio and photos on the filesystem, metadata in PostgreSQL), steadily building the labeled dataset that a future ML model will train on.

每次分析的样本和用户事后确认的标注都会留存（音频和照片存文件系统、元数据存 PostgreSQL），持续积累未来 ML 模型的标注训练集。

### Identity & Forced Update 身份与强制更新

No accounts: the app uses an anonymous device ID stored in the Keychain. Forced update is implemented on both sides: the server returns the latest and minimum supported client versions and evaluates updateAvailable / forceUpdate against the caller's currentVersion (configuration-driven). At launch the app fetches the policy from the selected server; on a version mismatch it shows a blocking update screen whose button jumps to the App Store link from the policy (updateUrl, to be filled in once the store listing exists). If the policy cannot be fetched, the app proceeds (fail-open).

不做账号体系：App 用保存在 Keychain 的匿名设备 ID。强制更新已双端实现：服务端返回最新与最低支持版本，并按调用方的 currentVersion 计算 updateAvailable / forceUpdate（配置驱动）；App 启动时向所选服务器拉取策略，版本不一致即显示不可关闭的更新页，按钮跳转到策略里的 App Store 链接（updateUrl，待上架后填入）。拉不到策略时放行（fail-open）。
